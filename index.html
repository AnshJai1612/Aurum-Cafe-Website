Our Story

The Meaning of
Aurum

Aurum — Latin for gold — is our promise. Not in palette alone, but in every grain of coffee, every fold of pastry, and every moment you spend with us in Al Furjan.

Born from a desire to create a space where the neighbourhood comes together — Aurum serves up Lebanese and Mediterranean cuisine, specialty grills, fresh salads, wood-fired pizza and artisan coffee, all under one roof in Discovery Gardens.
